What you can do with Flit

- Take Notes and Transcribe Voice Recordings

Write naturally or speak your thoughts, Flit instantly turns voice recordings into clean, structured notes.

- Organise your Notes

Sort notes into categories and build connections between them with bi-directional links. Every link shows you how your ideas relate and helps you navigate your knowledge with ease.

- Full Import & Export

Bring your existing notes from other apps or export everything whenever you want. Your data is never locked in.

- Multi-Device Sync and Backup

Your notes stay in perfect sync across web and your devices. Automatic backups give you total peace of mind.
