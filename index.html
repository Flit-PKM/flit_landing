Welcome to the Flit-PKM ecosystem

Flit is a comprehensive Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) and Wellness ecosystem providing private and secure knowledge management for individuals.


Vision


The Flit ecosystem is all about turning your everyday voice notes, meditations, and journal entries into a helpful "second brain" that grows with you. It listens to what you say, pulls out the key ideas, connects them in smart ways, and organizes everything from small details to big-picture insights. With free, easy-to-use apps on your phone, web, and for wellness, you can capture thoughts anytime and explore them naturally. The secure core uses AI to spot patterns, create connections, and help you refine it all.

Teamwork between your intuition and smart tech for better ideas, clearer memories, and those insightful "aha!" moments.
