Flit - Personal Note Taking and Knowledge Management

Flit is a clean, focused Note Taking and Personal Knowledge Management app; built to help you capture ideas quickly and keep them perfectly organized for the long term.

Whether you're jotting down meeting notes, transcribing a voice memo, or slowly growing your personal knowledge base; Flit makes the entire process simple, fast and dependable.

Flit is designed for people who want a straightforward, trustworthy tool that simply works. No clutter, no tracking, no data-sharing; just fast note-taking, smart organization, and reliable access everywhere.
